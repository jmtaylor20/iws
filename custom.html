What fully custom means

Every decision is yours to make.

Fully custom starts with a blank page. We measure the room, listen to how you live in it, and design cabinetry that belongs there and nowhere else. No catalog, no standard sizes, no "closest match."

You choose the wood, the door profile, the finish, the hardware, the interior fittings and the one-off details: an arched window seat, a hidden pantry door, a walnut library wall, a fireplace surround. If you can describe it, we can draw it. If we can draw it, we can build it.

How a custom project runs

More conversation up front. No surprises after.

01

Discover

We meet in your space, talk through how you use it, and look at inspiration together. Bring photos, sketches, napkin drawings, anything.

02

Design

Shop drawings of every elevation, plus wood and finish samples you can hold in the room. We revise until it's right.

03

Build

CNC-cut for precision, then joined, sanded and finished by hand in our Rancho Cucamonga shop under the eye of a master carpenter.

04

Install

Our own crew installs, scribes to your walls, and finishes on site. The people who built it are the people fitting it.

Signature or fully custom?

Same shop. Same hands. Two ways in.

Choose fully custom when the room is unusual, the design is one of a kind, or you want a specific wood, finish or detail with no compromises.

Choose Signature when you want IWS quality and a made-to-measure fit from a curated palette, at a price that's easier to plan around.

Not sure? Tell us about the project and we'll recommend the right path. Plenty of homes end up with a bit of both.
