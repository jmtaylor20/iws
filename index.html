Why Signature

Fully custom quality. Production-smart pricing.

Most people think they have two choices: big-box cabinets that never quite fit, or fully custom work that blows the budget.

Signature is the third option. We use the same hardwoods, joinery and hand finishing as our fully custom projects, but we standardize where you'll never notice. The quality goes up. The price comes down.

The Signature line

What you get, and what you don't pay for.

  • 01

    Built to your measurements

    Every Signature cabinet is cut for your space, not pulled from a warehouse shelf. Fillers, gaps and awkward corners are not part of the deal.

  • 02

    A curated palette

    A focused set of door styles, wood species and finishes we build again and again, so we build them fast and flawless.

  • 03

    The same hands

    The crew that builds our fully custom projects builds Signature. Same shop, same standards, same master carpenter checking the work.

  • 04

    Priced without the guesswork

    Straightforward pricing you can plan around, with no dealer or middleman between you and the people building it.

How it works

From first sketch to final install.

01

Consult

Tell us about the space, the look you're after and the number you need to hit. We'll tell you honestly what's possible.

02

Design

We measure, draw and spec every cabinet, then walk you through door styles, finishes and hardware.

03

Build

Panels are cut on our CNC for precision, then assembled, sanded and finished by hand in our shop.

04

Install

Our own crew installs, adjusts and finishes on site, so the last five percent is as good as the first ninety-five.

Since 1987

A family shop with a master carpenter at the bench.

Tony Minardi started IWS in 1987 after apprenticing under a master carpenter. Nearly forty years later, the shop is still family-run, still in Rancho Cucamonga, and still building every cabinet in-house, now with CNC precision behind the hand work.
