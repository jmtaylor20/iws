The Signature line by IWS

Signature

High-quality custom, on a budget.

Made-to-measure cabinetry from a curated palette of door styles, woods and finishes. Built in our shop, installed by our crew, priced for real life.

The honest truth about cabinet pricing

Custom doesn't have to mean unlimited.

Fully custom means every decision is wide open: any species, any profile, any finish, any hardware, any one-off detail. That freedom is wonderful, and it's expensive, because every choice means new setups, new samples and new time at the bench.

Signature keeps the decisions that matter, like fit, materials and finish quality, and simplifies the rest. You choose from a palette we know inside and out, and we build it exactly to your space.

How we keep the price down

Smart where it's invisible. Uncompromising where it shows.

  • 01

    A focused menu

    A curated set of door styles, wood species and finishes. Fewer variables means fewer surprises, faster builds and a better price.

  • 02

    CNC precision

    Boxes and panels are cut on our CNC to exact dimensions, then assembled and finished by the same hands that build our fully custom work.

  • 03

    Shop direct

    You work with the people building your cabinets. No showroom markup, no dealer in the middle, no subcontracted installers.

  • 04

    A streamlined process

    Measure, choose, build, install. We've refined the steps so your project moves efficiently from first meeting to finished room.

Questions

Straight answers about Signature.

Is Signature lower quality than fully custom?
No. Signature is built in the same shop, from the same materials, by the same people. The savings come from a curated palette and a streamlined process, not from cutting corners on construction or finish.
What can I customize?
Every cabinet is built to your measurements and layout. You choose from the Signature palette of door styles, wood species, finishes and hardware. If you want something outside the palette, our fully custom service is always available.
How long does a Signature project take?
It depends on the size of the project, and we'll give you a firm timeline with your estimate. Because the palette is focused and the process is refined, Signature typically moves faster than a fully custom build.
Do you install?
Yes. Our own crew installs everything we build, so the people who made your cabinets are the ones fitting them in your home.
Do you still do fully custom work?
Absolutely. Fully custom has been the heart of IWS since 1987 and it isn't going anywhere. Signature simply gives you a second, more budget-friendly way to get IWS quality.
Do you take on commercial projects?
Yes. We've built custom office spaces for medical suites and corporate offices alongside our residential work. Ask us about your space.
