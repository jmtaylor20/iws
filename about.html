Our story

Nearly four decades of building it right.

Tony Minardi began his carpentry career as an apprentice shortly after graduating from high school, spending the next several years learning from a master carpenter. In 1987 he branched out on his own and founded Interior Woodwork Specialist.

Since then, IWS has been providing Southern California with beautiful, quality custom cabinetry: kitchens, bathrooms, home offices, built-ins, and commercial spaces from medical suites to corporate offices.

The standards and methods of carpentry have changed over the years. Our commitment to quality, and to building a product that suits your needs and your life, never has.
